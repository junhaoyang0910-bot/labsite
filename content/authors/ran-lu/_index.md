Ran Lu is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science (LSGS) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He received his Ph.D. degree in Geodesy and Surveying Engineering from Wuhan University in 2026. His research interests include precise GNSS data processing, geophysical modeling and applications, and terrestrial reference frames.
