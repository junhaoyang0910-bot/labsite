Dachen Peng is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Land Surveying and Geo-Informatics (LSGI), The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He received his Bachelor&#39;s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Xi&#39;an Jiaotong University and his Ph.D. in Physics from Sun Yat-sen University. His research interests include LEO satellite precise orbit determination (POD) based on GNSS data, advanced research on LEO satellite dynamic modeling, and orbit maneuver thrust inversion.
