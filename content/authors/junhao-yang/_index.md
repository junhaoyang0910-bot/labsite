Junhao Yang is a PhD student in the Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science (LSGS) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He received his B.S. degree in Geographic Information Science from Southwest University and his M.S. degree in Geomatics from PolyU. His current research focuses on real-time precise orbit determination (POD).
