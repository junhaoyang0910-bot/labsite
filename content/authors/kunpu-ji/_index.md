Kunpu Ji is a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Land Surveying and Geo-Informatics (LSGI) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). He received his Bachelor&#39;s degree in Surveying Engineering from the School of Geomatics Science and Technology, Nanjing Tech University, Nanjing, China, in 2017, and his Master&#39;s and Doctoral degrees in Geodesy from the College of Surveying and Geo-Informatics, Tongji University, Shanghai, China, in 2020 and 2025, respectively. His main research interests include geodetic inverse problems, geodetic time series analysis, and GNSS quality control theory.
