Bin Xiang is a PhD student in the Department of Land Surveying and Geospatial Science (LSGS) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). She received her Bachelor's degree in Railway Traffic Signal and Control and Master's degree in Control Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University. Her research interests include LEO satellite navigation, as well as anti-jamming and anti-spoofing technologies for UAV navigation.
